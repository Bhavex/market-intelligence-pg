Privacy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

The short version

This site doesn't collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising.

Cookies and storage

No cookies are set, so there's no cookie banner. If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Third-party services

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads two open-source libraries (Chart.js and Leaflet) from the jsDelivr CDN and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Like any web server, these services can see your IP address and browser type when they deliver files. See the GitHub privacy statement.

The market data

The data shown is public end-of-day stock market data. It's for education only and isn't investment advice.
