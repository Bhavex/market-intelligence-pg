**A9 text (to insert).**
> *RSI zone* is +1 if the 14-day RSI is above 70, −1 if it is below 30 and 0 otherwise; exactly 30 or 70 is 0. The RSI uses simple 14-day averages of gains and losses, not Wilder smoothing, and is 50 if there were no moves and 100 if there were no losses. *Momentum sign* is the sign of price / price 20 sessions earlier − 1, computed from unrounded prices: +1, −1, or 0 on an exact tie; days without 20 prior sessions are excluded. A flip is a change of the +1/0/−1 value. *Strongest sector* is the sector, among the five dashboard sectors, with the highest equal-weight average 20-day momentum that day.
